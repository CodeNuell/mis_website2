About Mercy International

Our Mission

To provide a holistic, child-centered education that fosters intellectual curiosity, moral integrity, and a lifelong love for learning. We prepare students to excel in a rapidly changing world.

View Details

Our Vision

To be a centre of academic excellence where learners are empowered to become innovative thinkers and responsible global citizens, guided by the principle that knowledge is power.

Our Glorious History

Founded in 1995, Mercy International Schools has grown from a small nursery class to a full-fledged primary institution, serving over 1000 students across three campuses. Our legacy of excellence continues.
